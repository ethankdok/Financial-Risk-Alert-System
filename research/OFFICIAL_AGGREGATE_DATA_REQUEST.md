# Request for aggregate official data — draft, not sent

University research question: among reported investment scam incidents from 2024 through September 2026, how frequently were semiconductor, biotech, renewable energy and other industry narratives explicitly used?

Ask the competent agency whether annual/monthly anonymous aggregates exist for: distinct incident count, narrative industry codes (allow multiple codes per incident), unclassifiable share, applicable case-date definition and aggregate recorded losses. If industry tags were never recorded, ask whether a privacy-preserving academic dataset or official existing report could support manual coding. Request advice on deduplicating multiple news stories covering the same investigation.

Do not send without the project team's approval and correct institutional contact details. Until a suitable official denominator is available, describe results as *the distribution among the screened public reports only*, not nationwide incidence.

Data catalogs checked: https://data.gov.tw/dataset/38262 ; https://data.gov.tw/dataset/160055 ; https://data.gov.tw/dataset/78432 .
