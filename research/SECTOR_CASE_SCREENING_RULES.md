# Investment theme screening protocol

Period: public notices published 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-26. Unit: distinct underlying investigation, not news articles or website hits. Apply sector labels only where the underlying alleged investment theme is explicitly stated. Multi-theme investigations receive one incident ID and multiple tags, not multiple unique incidents. Keep source publication date separate from incident date. Exclude ordinary corporate earnings changes from allegations of fraud. Distinguish police-confirmed operations, prosecution allegations, and reports without verified industry. Do not infer national frequency from selectively published cases.

Data source distinction: police anti-fraud debunking data contains dated narrative releases, whereas blocked website datasets do not systematically establish investment industry. Government agency case-level sector cross-tabulations would be needed for any national rate estimate.

Short-term deliverable: compare only a consistently screened set of public releases, report numbers as examples within the collected sample, and document all excluded ambiguous cases. Request official aggregate counts before making a sector-prevalence claim.
